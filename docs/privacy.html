TinyCare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

TinyCare helps mothers and caregivers track postpartum recovery, children's growth, vaccinations, nutrition and activities.

By default TinyCare stores your information on your own device. If the version of the app you use is connected to a TinyCare server (the optional cloud sync feature, shown as "Cloud sync" in Settings), a copy is also kept on that server so you can use your account on more than one device. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Information the app stores on your device

Account details: your name, phone number and a salted, hashed form of your password. The hash and a session token are kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Health and care records you enter: delivery date and type, diet preference, allergies, blood pressure, weight, symptoms, mood, sleep and hydration logs, mood-check answers, children's names, dates of birth, measurements, vaccination records, completed activities and milestones.

App settings: language, region, theme, app-lock and reminder preferences.

How information is used

Your records are used only inside the app to show your recovery plan, vaccination schedule, growth history, nutrition ideas and alerts.

Vaccine reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Sharing

We do not share your records with anyone. If you choose to create a Health Passport PDF, the file is created on your device and you decide where to share it using your phone's share sheet.

If crash reporting is enabled in your version of the app, anonymous technical crash information (such as the error and device model) may be sent to our crash-reporting provider. It does not include the health records you enter, your name or your phone number.

Optional cloud sync (when connected to a TinyCare server)

What is sent: your name, phone number, a salted hash of your password, and the records you enter (profile, children, vaccinations, growth, recovery logs, mood logs, and language/region/theme preferences). The notification inbox, app-lock and reminder switches stay on the device.

Why: so the same account and records are available on your other devices and in the web version. The server operator can see your name and phone number; your health records are stored encrypted and are used only to return them to you.

Who runs the server: the organisation that published the version of the app you installed. If you do not want cloud sync, use a version without a server connection.

Children's information

TinyCare is meant to be used by parents and caregivers, not by children. Children's details are entered by an adult and stay on the adult's device.

Security

Credentials are kept in hardware-backed secure storage where available (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). In the web version the browser offers no equivalent secure storage, so please use the web version only on devices you trust. You can lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode.

The app talks to a TinyCare server only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and only when cloud sync is enabled. On the server your password is stored only as a salted hash, your health records are encrypted at rest, and sign-in attempts are rate limited. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please also protect your device with a screen lock.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can edit or remove any record in the app. To erase everything, open Settings and choose "Delete account and data". This permanently removes your account, records, reminders and settings from the device and, when cloud sync is enabled, from the server as well.

Without cloud sync there is no copy elsewhere, so we cannot recover lost data or a forgotten password. With cloud sync, deleting your account on one device removes the server copy; other devices signed in to the same account will lose access.

Medical disclaimer

TinyCare provides general information. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, contact your doctor or the nearest hospital.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
