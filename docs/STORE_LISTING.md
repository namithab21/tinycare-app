**Full description**
TinyCare is a private, offline health companion for mothers and little ones.

• Vaccination schedule with reminders, so no dose is missed
• Growth tracking with WHO-style reference ranges
• Safe, age-appropriate activities — gentle bonding and sensory play for under-6-month-olds
• Postpartum recovery plan: rest-first guidance for the first 90 days, extra care after C-section, blood-pressure and warning-sign alerts
• Regional recovery meals and child nutrition ideas, with allergy-aware substitutions
• Mood calendar and daily check-ins
• Health Passport PDF to share with your doctor
• English, हिन्दी, ಕನ್ನಡ, తెలుగు, தமிழ்; light and dark mode; Face ID / fingerprint lock

Your data stays on your phone by default. Optional encrypted cloud sync lets you use the same account on more devices and on the web. No ads, no tracking. Delete everything any time from Settings.
